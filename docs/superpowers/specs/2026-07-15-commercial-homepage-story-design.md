# FleetLever commercial homepage story

## Goal

Turn the commercial homepage into one continuous operational story. Each section must introduce the next step in FleetLever's release-control loop, demonstrate the relevant product capability, and move the buyer closer to a measured pilot.

## Story spine

1. **Promise**: know what can go out next and what cannot.
2. **Set the plan**: jobs, rentals, shifts, and required assets enter one release flow.
3. **See the operational truth**: one board shows ready, review, blocked, and ownership states.
4. **Act before the cutoff**: FleetLever detects a blocker, assigns it, supports replanning, and records the outcome.
5. **Release with proof**: the action queue, asset passport, and decision history form one auditable chain.
6. **Apply the loop**: concrete release questions show how the same control model serves four fleet types.
7. **Measure it**: a 30-day pilot measures readiness, resolution, replacement, and prevented failed releases.

## Content rules

- Keep the hero headline, but shorten the supporting sentence.
- Use numbered section eyebrows to make the progression explicit.
- Do not repeat the same release/evidence claim in multiple headings.
- Use one concise operational example throughout: CR-04, its missing certificate, its owner, replacement, and final decision.
- Use one primary CTA phrase: `Request a demo`.
- Use `See the release flow` for the secondary hero action.
- Describe use cases through the decision each operation needs to make, not broad industry claims.

## Layout and motion

- Preserve the dark photographic hero and final pilot band.
- Preserve alternating mist and white section backgrounds.
- Keep the readiness-lane animation, but reduce its scroll travel.
- Convert the existing How it works summary plus timeline into one timeline-led explanation.
- Introduce the proof section with one shared heading, then show action queue, asset passport, and decision history as three consecutive proof moments.
- Reduce the asset-passport animation travel so it remains an accent rather than a long pause.
- Keep the existing maximum content width and responsive gutters.

## Mobile behavior

- Keep the same narrative order.
- Crop product screenshots inside their preview frames so important operational information is legible before enlargement.
- Retain the full image in the enlargement dialog.
- Keep all copy and controls within the viewport without horizontal overflow.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Acceptance criteria

- The homepage reads as one release-control story from plan to measured result.
- Major section headings are unique.
- The phrase `The decision has the evidence behind it.` is removed.
- Desktop page height is at most 8,000px at 1440x1000.
- Mobile page height is at most 9,000px at 390x844.
- The five light sections continue to alternate mist/white/mist/white/mist.
- Product screenshots remain enlargeable and accessible.
- No console errors, broken images, serious accessibility violations, or horizontal overflow.
