# FleetLever v0.15.0

The public offer now matches the product.

## The contradiction

The landing page said **"Start 15-day trial."** The pricing page said **EUR 1,000 paid pilot, 12-month
commercial term, the pilot is not free.** Both could not be true.

The landing page was the honest one. `/signup` works, and migration `0005` gives every new account a
genuine 15-day trial with no card. It was the pricing page describing an offer the product had moved
past.

## Pricing page

- The EUR 1,000 founding pilot becomes the free 15-day trial.
- `Term: 12 months after the pilot` becomes `Trial: 15 days free, no card, no obligation`.
- The three FAQs — all written around the pilot and the 12-month term — are rewritten around the
  trial and the absence of a minimum term.
- Every pilot CTA now starts the trial instead of opening the demo form.

The EUR 6,000 / 12,000 / 24,000 annual scopes stay. They are real, and they are what gets sold once a
fleet has decided. They simply stop being the front door.

## Landing page

The hero leads on the moment rather than the category:

> Know tonight what can't go out tomorrow.

"Know what can go out next" had no time anchor and no consequence. Underneath it sits the one claim
competitors in this category cannot match — **no hardware, no installer, no sales call** — where most
vendors require installed devices and gate signup behind a demo form.

The primary CTA becomes "Start free". It already pointed at `/signup`; only the label was wrong.

## Structured data

The landing page carried the EUR 1,000 pilot inside its JSON-LD `Offer`, so search engines were being
told the entry price was EUR 1,000 regardless of what the visible page said. It is now a zero-price
15-day trial.

## Deliberately not included

Per-asset tiers, a free 3-asset plan and card-on-trial were all recommended by the go-to-market
review. None of them ship here, because there is no payment integration and no plan or asset-limit
enforcement anywhere in this codebase. Advertising them would recreate the exact contradiction this
release removes.

## Known gap

`public/fleetlever/site/tomorrow-readiness-dashboard.png`, used on both the landing and pricing
pages, still shows a worksite named "Athens Metro Extension". The console now calls it "Metro line
extension". The marketing screenshots need regenerating against the current English console.
