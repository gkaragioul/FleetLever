# FleetLever v0.14.0

One service, one app: fleetlever.com and the English console. Nothing Greek-specific is left
in what ships.

v0.13.0 removed the Greek public-sector products. This finishes the job on the parts that
were still market-specific.

## Marketing site

The municipal and public works industry is cut from the landing page, along with its
photography. The industry switchboard renumbers 01-04.

The hero fanned four scenes across the full width by absolute percentage, so removing one
would have left a visible gap. The remaining three are respaced to close it. Both the hero
and the switchboard were rendered and inspected rather than assumed.

## Product terminology

`KTEO`, the Greek roadworthiness test, becomes `Roadworthiness` across the document
categories, seed data and Copilot matching. The `category` column is plain text with no
constraint, so this is a rename and not a schema change.

## Defaults

`bootstrap-production-tenant` stamped `el-GR` and `Europe/Athens` onto every new production
tenant. That was the one place the Greek market would have kept reappearing no matter how much
copy was translated. It now creates `en-GB` and `Europe/London` tenants.

Athens depots, `Europe/Athens` and `el-GR` also leave the demo seed data.

Migration `0012` moves the `organizations` and `profiles` timezone defaults off
`Europe/Athens` for future rows. `0001_fleetlever_core.sql` is deliberately untouched: it has
already run in production, and editing an applied migration would not re-run in any case.

## Contract scripts

Three checks asserted the old world and were quietly wrong:

- `verify-console-edition` required the token `isMunicipalConsole` and the Greek string
  `Πακέτα εργασίας`.
- `verify-commercial-site` required the bilingual `lang` attribute.

They now assert English, and the console check fails outright if Greek script reappears in the
console component.
